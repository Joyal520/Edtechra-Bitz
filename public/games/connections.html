How to Play Connections

  • Find four groups of four words that share a hidden connection.
  • Select four tiles and tap Submit to check your guess.
  • If 3 of your 4 words belong to the same group, you'll see a "One away!" hint.
  • You have 4 mistakes before the remaining answers are revealed.
  • Use the Hint bulb to highlight 2 matching words, or the Search glass to reveal a category name.
